## Requirements rule

Each requirement should have:
- unique ID;
- priority;
- user value;
- acceptance criteria;
- dependency;
- test expectation;
- phase mapping.

The attached source PRD establishes the product direction and major features. Detailed engineering choices belong in the Architecture documentation and must not be silently treated as product requirements.

## Approval status

This master PRD becomes the final product contract only after explicit project-owner review and approval.
