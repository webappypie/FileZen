# Notifications, Monetization and Analytics

## Notifications Center

A bell icon provides access to a dedicated Notifications Center.

Notification types:
- manual/admin notifications;
- app-generated notifications based on meaningful FileZen events;
- system/integration notifications where appropriate.

Capabilities:
- read/unread state;
- timestamp;
- deep-link/action target where applicable;
- individual dismiss/clear;
- Clear All;
- safe behavior when notification delivery is unavailable.

Do not generate noisy notifications merely to increase engagement.

## Monetization

- Major FileZen functionality remains available to free users.
- Advertising is non-intrusive.
- Multiple ad providers may be used through the approved WAPCentral integration.
- A custom promotion system may promote the user's own apps.
- One-time purchase removes ads.
- Normal functionality does not require a subscription.

## Analytics

Collect only privacy-safe product telemetry necessary for stability and product decisions. Avoid file names, file contents, OCR text, private vault metadata, or unnecessary personal information.
